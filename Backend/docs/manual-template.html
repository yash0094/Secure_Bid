Finding your way around SecureBid

SecureBid runs three separate portals from one app — a bidder discovering and pricing tenders, a department publishing and awarding them, and an official watching the whole system for bid-rigging patterns. Each portal has its own colour, its own navigation, and its own job. This manual gets a first-time user of any one of them fluent, screen by screen.

01 Signing in

The logo plays for about three seconds on a genuinely fresh browser session, then drops you at your portal's sign-in page — a bookmarked or shared link skips straight past it on later visits. Each sign-in page carries its own colour and description, plus a pill switcher at the bottom to jump portals.

No account yet? Create an account asks a bidder for a company name; a tender caller or official additionally for a department and state. Or use a demo account straight away:

One session at a time. Signing in on a second tab or device silently signs out the first — SecureBid keeps exactly one active session per account, on purpose.
Continue with Google. The Bidder sign-in page also offers a real "Continue with Google" button (Google's own widget) when the server has it configured — SecureBid verifies the credential directly against Google before creating or signing you into a bidder account. No password to remember for that path. Tender Caller and Official accounts are institutional and use email/password only.
New accounts start unverified (Google sign-ins are pre-verified, since Google already confirmed the email). A banner offers to resend the link; without a mail server configured, the link is printed to the server console rather than emailed. The same applies to password-reset links, and a reset signs out every other session on the account.

02 How to navigate the app

SecureBid is built like a mobile app on purpose — short, targeted visits, not a document to scroll through. Every portal fixes its four most-used features to the bottom of the screen, always visible, and tucks everything else behind a slide-out menu.

RoleBottom bar (always visible)Menu (tap the hamburger icon)
BidderHome · Discover · Pipeline · Anomaly Portfolio, Analytics, Competitors, Eligibility Parser, Alerts & Saved Searches, Company Profile
Tender CallerTenders · Create · Awards · ProfileSign out only — all four features already fit the bar

Colour is also navigation: the top bar's dot, the active bottom-bar icon, and every primary button pick up your portal's colour, so two SecureBid tabs open in different roles are distinguishable at a glance.

RoleBidder / Contractor

Discover, price, and track a bid

Everything below lives behind the bottom bar and menu once you're signed in at /login.

1 Dashboard

Four tiles orient you immediately: expected value across your live bids, how much working capital is tied up in EMD, your win rate, and how many bids are active right now (plus how many tenders were automatically screened out as ineligible). Below that, a ranked opportunity list sorted by expected profit — tap any row to open it.

2 Discover

Search and filter every live tender by keyword, category, state, or eligibility. Flip on Eligible only to hide anything your profile doesn't qualify for, or tap Save this search to revisit it later from Alerts & Saved Searches.

3 The pricing recommendation

Open any tender and this is the core of the product. Enter your cost (or leave it blank for 82% of the estimated value) and a target margin, then tap Recommend a bid. You get a single recommended bid with its win probability and expected profit, a confidence label telling you how much historical data that estimate rests on, and three named strategies — Conservative, Recommended, and Aggressive — so the choice between margin and throughput is explicit rather than hidden inside one number.

4 Pipeline

A Kanban board across five stages — Watching, Preparing, Submitted, Won, Lost. Tap a card to expand it: cards being prepared show a prep checklist you can tick off or add to; cards that won show post-award milestones instead. If a tender caller awards a tender you bid on, your card moves to Won or Lost by itself.

5 Anomaly — the fourth bottom-bar feature

Six OECD-style structural screens for bid-rigging patterns — Bid Dispersion, L1–L2 Spread, Win Concentration, Bid Rotation, Repeated Pairing, and Round-Number Clustering — run across every buyer/category bucket and ranked by risk. It shares the bottom bar with Home, Discover and Pipeline because it's the app's other core differentiator, not an afterthought. Risk shows as Elevated / Watch / Normal — deliberately never "corrupt" or "clean" — and every flag explains itself in plain language.

6 Portfolio, Analytics & the rest (in the menu)

  • Portfolio — given your capital and a maximum bid count, optimises which open tenders to bid on for the best total expected profit, and shows the shadow price of one more lakh of capital.
  • Analytics — capital deployed vs. available, a win-rate trend, and value by category, built from your own pipeline history as it accumulates.
  • Competitors — who else bids in a bucket and how, and their win rate across categories.
  • Eligibility Parser — paste any clause text to see exactly what gets extracted and whether your profile meets it.
  • Alerts & Saved Searches and Company Profile — your notification centre, and the profile data that drives every eligibility check and pricing default above.
RoleTender Caller

Publish tenders, award a winner

Signed in at /login/buyer, as the procuring department or PSU. All four features fit the bottom bar — the menu here holds only Sign out.

1 Tenders & Create

Tenders lists everything you've authored with its status — Draft, Published, or Closed — and how many bids it's received. Create takes the basics (title, category, state, value, EMD, closing date); paste eligibility clause text into the last field and SecureBid runs the same parser bidders see, structuring your criteria automatically. Save as a draft to keep working, or publish to go live immediately.

2 Evaluating and awarding

Open a published tender to see every submission, lowest bid first. Shortlist or Reject to organise your review, or tap Award to pick a winner. Awarding closes the tender, records the award and every bid into the historical data the bidder-side pricing and anomaly-detection tools learn from, and flips every bidder's pipeline card to Won or Lost automatically — in one step that either completes fully or not at all.

A tender can only be awarded once. Trying again on an already-closed tender is rejected outright rather than silently recording a second award.
RoleGovernment Official

Watch the whole system for anomalies

Signed in at /login/official. Read-only by design — no bidding, no publishing. This portal is where the anomaly-detection system is most fully expressed.

1 Overview

System-wide totals — tenders, awards, pipeline value, awarded value — an award-value trend, spend by state, and the tender-calling organisations ranked by total value published.

2 Anomaly — system-wide detection

The same six structural screens bidders see, run across every buyer/category bucket from every tender caller, ranked by risk. This is the page a "smart governance / automated public procurement anomaly detection" workflow is actually about: surfacing which buyer/category combinations deserve a closer look, explaining why through six named, plain-language screens, and deliberately never labelling a vendor "corrupt" — these are statistical signals for deciding where to look, not findings.

3 Registry

Every tender from every caller — including drafts and closed ones, which a bidder's Discover page never shows — filterable by status. Nothing is hidden from this view.

Account & security

  • Single active session — only one sign-in is valid per account at a time. Signing in elsewhere silently replaces the earlier session rather than running both at once.
  • Real server-side sign-out — signing out invalidates your session token on the server immediately, not just in your browser.
  • Password resets revoke everything — resetting your password signs out every other active session on that account.
  • Google sign-in is verified, not trusted blindly — the credential is checked directly against Google's own servers before a session is created.

A short glossary

Indian public-procurement terms used throughout the app.

EMD — Earnest Money Deposit
A refundable deposit a bidder puts down to bid, forfeited if they win and back out. This is the real money "locked up" in the capital tiles.
L1
Shorthand for the lowest bid on a tender — the usual winner under price-only evaluation. "L1 ratio" means the winning bid as a fraction of the estimated value.
MSME / Udyam
Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises and their registration scheme; MSME-registered bidders often get relaxed turnover thresholds or EMD exemptions, which the eligibility parser accounts for automatically.
Bidder class — Class I / Class II
Local-content classification under the Make in India procurement order; some tenders restrict eligibility to a particular class or better.
Cover bidding
A collusion pattern where several bidders submit deliberately uncompetitive bids so a pre-agreed member wins — what the anomaly screens are built to surface signs of.

Troubleshooting (running it yourself)

  • Blank page after sign-in? Build the frontend (npm run build inside frontend/) before starting python run.py — the backend serves those built files.
  • No "Continue with Google" button? It needs a real Google OAuth 2.0 Web Client ID — create one in Google Cloud Console under APIs & Services → Credentials, add this app's URL as an Authorized JavaScript origin, then set GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID in the server's environment. Without it, the button is hidden and email/password still works.
  • Verification or reset email never arrives? Without SMTP_HOST configured, SecureBid logs the email (link included) to the server console instead of sending it.
  • Want a clean demo dataset again? python run.py --reseed wipes the database and regenerates the synthetic corpus, including the three demo accounts above.
